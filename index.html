How Our Athletic Performance Intelligence Works

Athletic Performance Intelligence is designed to integrate with any wearables or function independently without a wearable technology, enabling scalable use across sports organizations regardless of their current technology adoption.

Unified Data Processing

Regardless of source, all data flows into FloBrain, which standardizes inputs, processes them across multiple dimensions, and applies Athletic Performance Intelligence models: readiness, load, recovery, risk, performance, and context.

Insight Delivery

FloBrain processes results into athlete and coach dashboards and automated reports, delivered as real-time insights, actionable recommendations, and continuous performance tracking.
